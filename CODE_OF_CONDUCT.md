# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to foster an open, respectful, and collaborative environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Focus on technical issues, not personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment or abusive language
- Discrimination or exclusionary behavior
- Trolling, intimidation, or deliberate disruption

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove or reject contributions that violate this code.
